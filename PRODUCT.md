## Platform

web

## Users

**Primary (the meeting):** orthopaedic and arthroscopy surgeons, sports physicians, and postgraduate orthopaedic trainees in India — the Hall A audience. **Second hall:** sports physiotherapists, strength & conditioning coaches, athletic trainers, nutritionists, exercise physiologists and team doctors.

**For the pamphlet surface specifically:** the people who receive it in WhatsApp doctor and association groups, and heads of department / PG coordinators who get it as an email attachment and may pin or circulate it. They see it on a phone, usually first as a thumbnail in a chat list, and decide in about a second whether to open it.

## Product Purpose

SIC 2027 — the Hyderabad Sports Injuries Conclave — is a two-day sports-injury meeting on **30 & 31 January 2027 (Saturday and Sunday)** in Hyderabad, inaugural edition, themed **Return to Sport**. Organised by Precision Orthopaedics LLP, in association with Apollo Hospitals, Financial District.

Success for the conclave is a meeting clinicians judge worth travelling to. Success for the pamphlet is narrower and confirmed by the organiser: **save the date** — the reader registers the name, the dates and the city, and remembers the conclave is happening. It is not a registration driver and not a sponsor pitch.

## Positioning

Hall A runs as **case-based debate, not lectures**: a chair presents one real patient, three surgeons argue genuinely different management positions on that same patient, then a physiotherapist takes the rehabilitation arc and a strength & conditioning coach takes return to performance, followed by 20–30 protected minutes in which the floor puts the same case back to the whole panel.

Research against AOSSM, ESSKA and ISAKOS programmes found no published meeting running the same surgeon + physiotherapist + S&C same-case format; the nearest precedents (AOSSM's case-based instructional course, ESSKA's hot-topic debate) are narrower. The rehabilitation and S&C voice in every block is the meeting's real differentiator — a benchmarked industry meeting of comparable size carried roughly three rehabilitation-led talks in total.

## Operating Context

The pamphlet travels two ways only, both confirmed: **forwarded on WhatsApp and social**, and **emailed to institutions**. It is not produced by a print shop and is not designed as a wall poster.

At the time it circulates: registration is not open, the venue is not announced, and no faculty are confirmed. The conclave is an annual franchise, not a one-off, so the 2027 edition establishes a visual identity later editions inherit.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static HTML, CSS and vanilla JS on Vercel; no build step and no framework. Print and social artefacts are produced by rendering HTML (print-to-PDF and element screenshots), so a pamphlet is a web document first.
- **CME/CPD is applied for, never "approved"** — Telangana State Medical Council. Copy must not upgrade this.
- **No faculty name appears on public material until that person has personally confirmed.** As of October 2026 none has; the public convention is "faculty announcement to follow".
- Venue is "to be announced"; dates and city are settled.
- Format for this surface is the designer's call: the organiser confirmed one format is enough.

## Brand Commitments

- Names: **SIC 2027**, **Hyderabad Sports Injuries Conclave**. Strapline **Evidence · Innovation · Excellence**. Theme **Return to Sport**.
- Palette is settled: navy `#14213D` with gold `#FCA311`. Ink-safe gold variants exist for light backgrounds because full gold fails contrast on white.
- Typefaces are settled: **Bebas Neue** display, **Archivo** body, both self-hosted.
- Attribution: "in association with Apollo Hospitals, Financial District" — association, not host. Organised by Precision Orthopaedics LLP (a separate entity from Agile Ortho; never use that branding here).
- Contact is `precisionortho4488@gmail.com`; site is `sportsinjuries.care`.
- The organiser rejected AI-generated decorative artwork for this project; do not reintroduce it.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real photography in `assets/` (hero image, sport imagery) and frame sequences in `flight/`.
- A QR code to sportsinjuries.care, a public brochure PDF, and the full scientific programme (two halls, nine case blocks).
- **Absences that must not be fabricated:** no confirmed faculty, no delegate numbers, no past-edition history (this is the inaugural), no venue, no testimonials, no accreditation approval.

## Product Principles

1. **Claim only what is true today.** Applied, not approved; announcement to follow, not a name list. The programme's credibility is the product.
2. **The rehabilitation and S&C voice is the differentiator** — surfaces that describe the meeting should carry it, not bury it under surgical content.
3. **Phone first for anything that circulates.** The pamphlet is read in a chat thread, not on a desk.
4. **Say less on outward material.** The brochure and website carry depth; circulating material exists to be recognised and remembered.
5. **Built to be inherited.** Each edition's material should still look right with a new year on it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No formal standard was set by the organiser, but two project-specific requirements are established: text must stay legible against the photographic backgrounds used in brand material, and the gold must switch to its ink-safe variant on light backgrounds, where full gold measures about 2:1 against white.
