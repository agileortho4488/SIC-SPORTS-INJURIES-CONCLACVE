WHY EXHIBIT AT HSC 2027

ONE ROOM. THE WHOLE
SPORTS INJURY TEAM.

HSC 2027 brings together the clinicians who choose, use and recommend sports-injury products every day — orthopaedic and arthroscopy surgeons, sports physicians, physiotherapists, and strength & conditioning professionals, alongside the next generation of postgraduate trainees.

In association with Apollo Hospitals, Financial District, the conclave pairs a focused scientific programme with a single exhibition hall that both scientific halls empty into at every shared break — surgeons, physiotherapists and S&C coaches walking the same floor.

2 DAYS30th & 31st January 2027 — Saturday & Sunday, Hyderabad
5 SPECIALTIESsurgeons, sports physicians, physios, S&C coaches, PG students
1 EXHIBITIONa single hall where every delegate breaks, meets and networks

PROSPECTUS & PRICING

Sponsorship and stall pricing is set out in the exhibitor & partnership prospectus — download it below; final stall sizes follow the venue floor plan, and stalls are allocated in the order bookings are confirmed. Product sessions and demonstrations sit in the exhibition area, outside the CPD programme. There are no sponsored sessions, logos or seat covers inside the scientific halls, no company-hosted meals and no delegate gifts — the programme stays independent and your compliance sign-off stays simple.
