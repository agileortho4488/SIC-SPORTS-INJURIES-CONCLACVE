WHY EXHIBIT AT HSC 2027

ONE ROOM. THE WHOLE
SPORTS INJURY TEAM.

HSC 2027 brings together the clinicians who choose, use and recommend sports-injury products every day — orthopaedic and arthroscopy surgeons, sports physicians, physiotherapists, and strength & conditioning professionals, alongside the next generation of postgraduate trainees.

In association with Apollo Hospitals, Financial District, the conclave pairs a focused scientific programme with a dedicated trade exhibition, live surgical video sessions, and hands-on engagement between faculty and industry.

2 DAYS30th & 31st January 2027 — Saturday & Sunday, Hyderabad
5 SPECIALTIESsurgeons, sports physicians, physios, S&C coaches, PG students
1 EXHIBITIONa single hall where every delegate breaks, meets and networks
