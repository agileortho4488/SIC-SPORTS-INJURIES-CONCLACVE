03

CME CREDIT POINTS

WHAT WE NEED FROM YOU FOR CME

CME credit points have been applied for with the Telangana State Medical Council. As per current TSMC requirements, the delegate list — with each doctor's state medical council registration number and registered mobile number — must be submitted in advance for credit hours to be awarded. Registration will therefore ask for your council registration details; please keep them handy.

Under NMC guidelines, renewal of medical registration requires accumulated CME/CPD credit hours — applications for renewal in 2027 require 24 credit hours in Telangana. Credit points for SIC 2027 remain "applied" until confirmed in writing by TSMC.
