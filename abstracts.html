FORMAT

HOW TO WRITE IT

Structured abstracts only. Submissions that exceed the word limit or skip sections will be returned for revision.

Structure — max 250 words

  1. Title — concise, no abbreviations
  2. Introduction — the question, in 2–3 sentences
  3. Methods — design, cohort, outcome measures
  4. Results — numbers, not adjectives
  5. Conclusion — one clinical takeaway
  • The presenting author must be a registered delegate of SIC 2027.
  • State the category and preferred format (paper / e-poster) at submission; the scientific committee's allocation is final.
  • One presenting author may submit a maximum of two abstracts.
  • Work must be original and not presented at a prior national meeting of the same scale.
  • PG students and young consultants are eligible for the Young Surgeon Forum and its awards.
