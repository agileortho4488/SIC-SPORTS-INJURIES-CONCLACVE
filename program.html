SCIENTIFIC PROGRAM

RETURN
TO SPORT

Return to sport is the theme of SIC 2027. Every session answers one question from a different angle — how do we get this athlete back, safely and at full performance? The conclave runs in two parallel halls, with the sports-science and nutrition content woven into the sessions where it belongs rather than separated into its own track, and joint plenary sessions that bring both halls together.

HALL ASurgery & Sports Orthopaedics

Orthopaedic & arthroscopy surgeons, sports physicians, PG trainees

HALL BRehabilitation, Physiotherapy & Strength and Conditioning

Sports physiotherapists, S&C coaches, athletic trainers, sports scientists
